East Anglia THREE — Project Finance Model

This personal project was born out of interest of project finance and a desire to explore the mechanics of infrastructure investing. East Anglia Three is an offshore wind farm within the East Anglia Array zone with East Anglia ONE and TWO, off the east coast of the United Kingdom. It is currently in its construction stage, supported by equity co-investors Iberdrola and Masdar.

All inputs are either officially-disclosed public information, retrieved from official government websites, or assumptions derived from published reports. 
The project reached financial close on 9 July 2025, backed by a £3.6bn senior debt facility from a 24-bank syndicate, with a £500m guarantee from EIFO (Denmark's ECA). Commercial Operation Date is expected in EOY2026.  

Although the debt size of the project is publicly disclosed, plugging in a dead number into a project finance model devalues the purpose of the model. The methodology employed was to use the debt to capital %  to determine pro-rata construction funding, and debt to be sized through goal seek targeting a minimum of 1.10 DSCR on Low Capacity Factor Case. 

Debt sizing came out to be GBP3.1B, GBP500M shy of the disclosed GBP3.6B. 


Limitations:
- Single senior debt issue - EIFO-guarantee of GBP500M would possibly offer debt at lower margins. This could have contributed to the discrepancy between the model's GBP3.1B and the disclosed GBP3.6B
- Other debt issues (e.g., subordinated debt) not disclosed, hence not modelled. 
- Amazon PPA details are not publicized and not modelled into revenue. 
- No decommissioning reserve
- No documentation on capacity factors of turbine model (SG 14-236 DD), no historical data on long-term performance and maintenance costs
- Operating timeline is split into semi-annual periods, does not account for seasonality
